Security & Data Protection

Your career data
should stay yours.

Job-helper is designed to process only the information required to provide job matching, resume analysis and AI-powered career assistance.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

How your data is handled

Job-helper follows a simple data-processing flow.

01

You browse

You open a job on a supported recruitment website.

02

Extract

Job-helper identifies relevant job description information.

03

Process

Required information is processed by our application and AI services.

04

Analyze

The system generates matching results and recommendations.

05

Return

Results are returned to the Job-helper interface.

Data minimization

We follow a data-minimization approach. Job-helper processes information only when it is necessary to provide a feature requested by the user.

JD

Job descriptions

Used to extract responsibilities, requirements, skills, experience and other relevant information.

CV

Resume data

Used to compare your experience and skills with the requirements of a specific job.

AI

AI processing

Used to generate matching analysis, skill-gap analysis and resume suggestions.

Browser permissions

Job-helper may request permission to access pages on supported recruitment websites. This allows the extension to identify and process job descriptions.

What this permission is used for

  • Reading relevant job description content.
  • Identifying job titles, responsibilities, requirements and related information.
  • Displaying Job-helper's analysis on the page.

What Job-helper does not do

  • We do not intentionally collect passwords.
  • We do not intentionally collect payment card information.
  • We do not sell browsing history to advertisers.
  • We do not submit job applications on your behalf unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

Encryption & secure transmission

Data transmitted between Job-helper clients and our backend services is designed to use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

API credentials and sensitive service configuration are intended to remain on our server-side infrastructure rather than being exposed directly in the browser extension.

Your API credentials are not stored in the extension.

Where third-party AI or translation services are used, requests are routed through controlled backend infrastructure when technically applicable.

AI & third-party processing

Some Job-helper features rely on third-party AI, translation, cloud infrastructure or other service providers.

Depending on the feature you use, information such as job descriptions or resume content may be transmitted to these providers for processing.

AI processing principles

  • AI processing is performed to provide the feature requested by the user.
  • We do not intentionally use submitted resumes for advertising purposes.
  • We aim to minimize the amount of information sent to external services.
  • Third-party providers may have their own security and privacy policies.

Data retention

We aim to retain user information only for as long as necessary to provide the requested service.

Data Purpose Retention
Job description Job analysis and matching Limited / feature dependent
Resume Resume matching and optimization Limited / feature dependent
Analysis results Providing requested results and history Feature dependent
Technical logs Security and debugging Limited retention

Infrastructure security

Job-helper uses cloud infrastructure and application security practices appropriate for an early-stage software service.

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted communication.
  • Server-side protection of API credentials.
  • Access control for backend systems.
  • Limited data retention.
  • Monitoring and error logging for service reliability.
  • Regular dependency and application maintenance.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. We continuously evaluate and improve our security practices as the product evolves.

You control your data

Your resume and career information remain yours. Job-helper does not acquire ownership of content that you submit to the service.

Depending on the features available in your account, you may be able to delete saved resumes, analysis history or other stored information.

You may also contact us to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

Responsible AI

Job-helper uses AI to assist users with job discovery, resume analysis and career-related decisions.

AI-generated results may contain errors or inaccuracies. A match score is an analytical estimate and does not represent an employer's decision, hiring probability or guarantee of employment.

AI assists your decision. It does not make the decision for you.

We encourage users to review AI-generated results before relying on them for employment or career decisions.

Security reporting

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in Job-helper, please report it to us privately rather than publicly disclosing the issue.

Please include enough information for us to reproduce and investigate the issue.
