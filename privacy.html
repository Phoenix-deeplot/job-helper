Job-helper 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-09-01

Job-helper（以下简称"Job-helper"、"我们"或"本服务"）重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明 Job-helper 如何收集、使用、存储、处理和保护您在使用 Job-helper 浏览器扩展、网站及相关服务（以下简称"服务"）时提供的信息。

使用 Job-helper，即表示您已阅读、理解并同意本隐私政策。

1. 我们收集的信息

根据您使用的功能，我们可能收集以下信息。

1.1 职位描述信息

当您使用 Job-helper 分析招聘职位时，我们可能处理您当前页面中的职位信息，包括：

  • 职位名称
  • 职位描述
  • 岗位职责
  • 任职要求
  • 技能要求
  • 工作经验要求
  • 学历要求
  • 工作地点
  • 薪资信息（如页面提供）

我们仅在提供职位分析、匹配和相关功能所必要的范围内处理这些信息。

1.2 简历信息

如果您主动上传、粘贴或输入简历，我们可能处理其中的信息，包括：

  • 工作经历
  • 教育经历
  • 技能
  • 项目经历
  • 语言能力
  • 职业经历
  • 其他您主动提供的简历内容

Job-helper 不会未经您的主动操作主动收集您设备中的其他文件或个人资料。

1.3 账户信息

如果未来功能需要账户注册，我们可能收集：

  • 电子邮箱
  • 账户标识
  • 订阅信息

我们不会出售您的个人信息。

1.4 技术信息

为了保障服务稳定性和安全性，我们可能收集有限的技术信息，例如：

  • 浏览器类型
  • 插件版本
  • 操作系统
  • 基础诊断信息
  • 错误日志

我们不会以此建立与您的真实身份无关的跨网站行为画像。

2. 我们如何使用信息

我们使用相关信息主要用于：

  • 分析职位描述；
  • 提取职位职责和要求；
  • 计算简历与职位之间的匹配程度；
  • 分析技能差距；
  • 提供简历优化建议；
  • 提供职位筛选和推荐功能；
  • 改善产品性能和用户体验；
  • 处理错误和技术问题；
  • 防止滥用、欺诈和安全风险；
  • 提供您主动请求的其他服务。

我们不会将您的简历或职位分析内容出售给招聘公司、广告商或数据经纪商。

3. AI 数据处理

Job-helper 使用人工智能模型处理部分用户请求。

根据您使用的具体功能，您主动提交的职位描述、简历内容或相关文本可能被发送至第三方 AI 服务商进行模型处理。

第三方服务商可能包括：

  • AI 模型提供商
  • 翻译服务提供商
  • 云基础设施提供商
  • 数据处理服务提供商

我们仅在提供相关功能所必要的范围内向这些服务商传输数据。

我们不会允许第三方 AI 服务商将您的简历用于与 Job-helper 无关的广告或用户画像。

4. 第三方服务

Job-helper 可能使用第三方基础设施和服务，例如：

  • 云托管服务
  • AI 模型 API
  • 翻译 API

  • 支付服务

5. 浏览器权限

Job-helper 可能请求访问您正在浏览的招聘网站页面。

该权限主要用于：识别职位描述 → 提取职位信息 → 执行职位匹配和相关功能。

  • 代表您修改账户信息。

Job-helper 的网页访问能力仅用于提供您主动使用的功能。

6. 数据保存

我们遵循数据最小化原则。

Job-helper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-09-01

Job-helper ("Job-helper", "we", or "this Service") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Job-helper collects, uses, stores, processes, and protects the information you provide when using the Job-helper browser extension, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using Job-helper, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information.

1.1 Job Description Information

When you use Job-helper to analyze a job posting, we may process job information on your current page, including:

  • Job title
  • Job description
  • Responsibilities
  • Requirements
  • Skills
  • Experience requirements
  • Education requirements
  • Location
  • Salary information, if available

We process this information only to the extent necessary to provide job analysis, matching, and related features.

1.2 Resume Information

If you actively upload, paste, or enter a resume, we may process information contained in it, including:

  • Work experience
  • Education
  • Skills
  • Project experience
  • Language proficiency
  • Career history
  • Other resume content you actively provide

Job-helper does not proactively collect other files or personal data on your device without your active action.

1.3 Account Information

If future features require account registration, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Subscription information

We do not sell your personal information.

1.4 Technical Information

To maintain service stability and security, we may collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Browser type
  • Extension version
  • Operating system
  • Basic diagnostic information
  • Error logs

We do not use this to build a cross-site behavioral profile unrelated to your real identity.

2. How We Use Information

We use relevant information primarily to:

  • Analyze job descriptions;
  • Extract job responsibilities and requirements;
  • Calculate the match between a resume and a job;
  • Analyze skill gaps;
  • Provide resume optimization suggestions;
  • Provide job filtering and recommendation features;
  • Improve product performance and user experience;
  • Handle errors and technical issues;
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and security risks;
  • Provide other services you actively request.

We do not sell your resume or job analysis content to recruiting companies, advertisers, or data brokers.

3. AI Processing

Job-helper uses artificial intelligence models to process some user requests.

Depending on the features you use, information you submit may be sent to third-party AI service providers for processing.

Third-party providers may include:

  • AI model providers
  • Translation providers
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Data processing providers

We only transmit data to these providers to the extent necessary to provide the relevant feature.

We do not allow third-party AI service providers to use your resume for advertising or user profiling unrelated to Job-helper.

4. Third-Party Services

Job-helper may use third-party infrastructure and services, such as:

  • Cloud hosting
  • AI model APIs
  • Translation APIs
  • Analytics services
  • Authentication services
  • Payment services

These third-party providers may process relevant information according to their own privacy policies.

By using Job-helper, you understand that, in order to provide relevant features, some data may need to be processed through third-party infrastructure.

5. Browser Permissions

Job-helper may request access to the recruiting website pages you are browsing.

This permission is primarily used for: identifying job descriptions → extracting job information → performing job matching and related features.

Job-helper does not, as a result of obtaining webpage access permission:

  • Read your passwords;
  • Read bank card information;
  • Read other webpage content unrelated to job analysis;
  • Apply for jobs on your behalf without your request;
  • Send messages on your behalf;
  • Modify your account information on your behalf.

Job-helper's webpage access capability is used only to provide features you actively use.

6. Data Retention

We follow the principle of data minimization.

For data generated by a single completed analysis, we retain it only for as long as necessary to provide the service.

If certain features require long-term retention, such as:

  • Analysis history
  • Saved jobs
  • Resume versions
  • User account data

we will clearly explain this within the relevant feature.

You may delete relevant data through means provided by the product, or contact us to request deletion.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including but not limited to:

  • HTTPS/TLS transmission
  • Access control
  • Authentication controls
  • Limited data retention
  • Server-side API key protection
  • Monitoring and logging

However, please note that no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights:

  • Access your personal information;
  • Request correction of personal information;
  • Request deletion of personal information;
  • Withdraw consent;
  • Restrict specific data processing;
  • Obtain a copy of your personal data;
  • Stop using the relevant service.
